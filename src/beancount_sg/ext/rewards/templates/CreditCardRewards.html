How to set up

Add rewards metadata to a credit card's open directive. A cashback card needs only its rates:

2020-01-01 open Liabilities:CreditCard:CashPlus USD
  rewards: "cashback"
  rewards-rate: 1.5            ; the default rate (defaults to 1)
  rewards-rate-groceries: 3    ; a category's rate: rewards-rate-<category>

A points card can also name its points and give them a cash value:

2020-01-01 open Liabilities:CreditCard:Voyager USD
  rewards: "points"
  rewards-rate-dining: 3
  rewards-unit: "VP"           ; optional, defaults to POINTS
  rewards-point-value: 0.0125  ; optional: a point's cash value
  • A rate of 3 means 3% back for cashback, or 3 points per unit spent for points. Naming a program's points (rewards-unit) keeps them from being added to other programs' points in the totals.
  • Spending is a card's charges to Expenses accounts, grouped by the card issuer's category for each purchase, e.g. "Dining", which can differ from the expense account it's booked to. It's read from the posting's category metadata, or else the transaction's; rewards-category-key: "bank_category" on the card reads another key instead. Spending without a category is uncategorized and earns the default rate.
  • Refunds count as negative spending. Payments from an Assets account never count, but card payments booked to an expense account look like refunds, so list those accounts (each with its subaccounts) in the extension's config: 
    2020-01-01 custom "fava-extension" "beancount_sg.ext.rewards" "{
  'exclude_accounts': ['Expenses:LoanPayments:CreditCardPayment'],
}"
  • Category rate keys are the category in lowercase with dashes: Food and Drink uses rewards-rate-food-and-drink.
  • Only transactions in the current Fava filter (e.g. the time range) are counted. Clicking a category, in the table or the charts, opens the card's account page filtered to it.
  • The charts show spend and the rewards' cash value by card, then category. Points count towards value only with a rewards-point-value.
