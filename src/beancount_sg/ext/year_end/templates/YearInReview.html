How accounts are classified

Each asset and liability account's cash-flow-role, as used above. Declare one on the account's open directive, where it also applies to subaccounts:

2020-01-01 open Assets:Bank:CD
  cash-flow-role: "investing"

Without one, assets are cash if their name contains Cash, Checking, Savings or Bank, else investing; liabilities are operating (e.g. credit cards, not debt) if their name contains Card, else financing (loans).

How to use
  • The review shows up to five years, ending with the last year of Fava's time filter, or this year without one. Other filters don't apply: it always covers whole years of the whole ledger.
  • Values are in the first operating currency, rounded to whole units: holdings at market value at each year's end (or at cost without a price), income and expenses at their value on each transaction's date.
  • Changes from the previous year are green when they're good (e.g. more savings, less spending or debt) and red when they're bad. A year-to-date year's income and spending aren't compared with whole years.
  • Spending / net worth is a year's expenses as a share of net worth at its start, like a withdrawal rate in retirement. Years of spending is its inverse, at the year's end (25 years is a 4% rate).
  • Click a year for its income statement, a category or loan for its account page, or a bar in the charts to make that the final year.
