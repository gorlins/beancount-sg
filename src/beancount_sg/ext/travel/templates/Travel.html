How to use
  • Travel spending is the expense postings of transactions tagged #travel, in the current Fava filter; refunds count negatively.
  • A transaction's trip is its tag ending in a year, e.g. #miami-2025, as the beancount_sg.plugins.tag_travel plugin adds. To recognize trip tags differently, give the extension a regex: 2020-01-01 custom "fava-extension" "beancount_sg.ext.travel" "{'trip_pattern': '^trip-'}". A transaction with several trip tags counts towards the first, alphabetically.
  • Travel spending without a trip tag is called out above, and shown as "no trip".
  • The chart shows the spending By trip then expense account, or By category (expense account) then trip; the icicle view shows the hierarchy most clearly.
  • Clicking a trip, in the table or the chart, opens the income statement filtered to its transactions. Clicking an expense account in the chart opens its page, filtered to that trip's travel transactions (or to all travel, when not under a trip).
