How to use
  • Travel spending is the expense postings of transactions tagged #travel, in the current Fava filter; refunds count negatively.
  • A transaction's trip is its trip tag, e.g. #miami-2025, as the beancount_sg.plugins.tag_travel plugin adds. Trips are the tags used on #travel transactions and on no others, so a tag like #recurring that's also used without #travel isn't a trip (and neither is a trip tag used even once without #travel). A travel transaction should have only one trip tag; one with several counts towards the first, alphabetically, and is listed in a warning above.
  • Travel spending without a trip tag is called out above, and shown as "no trip".
  • The chart shows the spending By trip then expense account, or By category (expense account) then trip; the icicle view shows the hierarchy most clearly. Over time shows the spending in each period, stacked by trip; choose the period (e.g. monthly) in the chart's interval menu.
  • Clicking a trip, in the table or the chart, opens the income statement filtered to its transactions. Clicking an expense account in the chart opens its page, filtered to that trip's travel transactions (or to all travel, when not under a trip). In Over time, a trip's part of a bar opens its income statement, and a bar's date limits the report to that period.
