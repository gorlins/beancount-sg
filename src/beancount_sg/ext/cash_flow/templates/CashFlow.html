How accounts are classified

Each account's cash-flow-role: cash accounts are the cash the statement explains, and every other account's flows are a line in its role's section. Declare a role on the account's open directive, where it also applies to subaccounts:

2020-01-01 open Assets:MoneyMarket
  cash-flow-role: "cash"

Without one, assets named with Cash, Checking, Savings or Bank are cash, other assets investing, liabilities and equity financing, and income and expenses operating: the same convention as Beancount.io's cash-flow report.

How to use
  • The period is Fava's time filter, or this year to date without one. Fava's account and advanced filters apply, as on Fava's own reports.
  • Each line is an account's effect on cash: money in is positive (green), money out negative (red). Income and expenses are rolled up to their top-level category, e.g. Expenses:Food.
  • Credit cards are liabilities, so financing by default: spending on a card is operating spending, borrowed from the card (financing), and paying it off repays that. Declare cash-flow-role: "operating" on a card to keep it in operating, where card spending and payments net out.
  • Values are in the first operating currency, rounded to whole units: flows at each transaction's date, cash at market value at the start and end. A change in value line shows any difference, e.g. from exchange rates.
  • Click a line or cash account for its account page, or part of a bar in the chart for the income statement (operating) or balance sheet.
