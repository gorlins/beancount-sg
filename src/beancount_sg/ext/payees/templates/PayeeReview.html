How to use
  • A payee's categories are the income and expense accounts its transactions (in the current Fava filter) post to; a split transaction counts in each. A payee is listed below only if its transactions don't all have the same categories, so one that always splits the same way isn't. Transactions without a payee are left out.
  • Payees in more than one category lists each payee's main category (the one with the most transactions) and its others. Payees almost always in one category, with the highest main share, come first: their other categories are the likeliest mistakes.
  • Similar payee names groups payees whose names are alike, ignoring case, punctuation and word order (e.g. SQ *CORNER COFFEE 0412 and Corner Coffee). Some groups will be different payees with similar names.
  • Click a payee or a category to open its transactions. With the Batch Edit extension enabled, Recategorize and Rename open it with the fix filled in, to preview before applying.
