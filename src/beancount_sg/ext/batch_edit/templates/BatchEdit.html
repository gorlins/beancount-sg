Batch Edit

How to use
  • Find transactions by payee and/or narration with a regular expression. Matching ignores case and starts at the beginning of the text: coffee finds "Coffee Shop", and .*coffee finds "coffee" anywhere. Only transactions in the current Fava filter (time, account, tags...) are searched.
  • New payee replaces the payee of every match. Use {key} to fill in a value from the transaction's metadata, e.g. {plaid_payee}; matches without that key keep their payee.
  • New category replaces the transaction's income or expense account. Transactions split across several categories show -SPLIT-: their payee can change, but their category is left alone.
  • Leave a field empty to keep it as it is. Payees can't be removed here; click a date to open the transaction and edit it directly.
  • Search previews the changes without editing anything; the search is kept in the page's address, so reloading or coming back keeps it. Apply edits your ledger files, changing only the payee and category text, then lists the transactions it edited as they are now (with links to open them), since they may no longer match the search.
