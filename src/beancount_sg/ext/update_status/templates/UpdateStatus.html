How to use

Track an account by adding Fava's fava-uptodate-indication metadata to its open directive (the beancount_sg.plugins.uptodate_subaccounts plugin extends it to an account's direct subaccounts):

2020-01-01 open Assets:Bank:Checking USD
  fava-uptodate-indication: TRUE
  • The status dot is green if the account's last entry is a passing balance assertion, yellow if it's a transaction, and red if it's a failing balance assertion. Click a yellow or red dot to copy balance directives for today.
  • Last balance is the amount of the account's last balance assertion in each currency, passing or not. Difference is the ledger's balance minus that: blank for a green dot, the failing assertion's difference for red, and the current balance minus the last balance for yellow (what a new assertion would need to confirm).
  • Balanced as of is the date of the account's last passing balance assertion, which checks the balance at the start of that day; an account that's never been balanced shows the date it was opened. Up to date as of is the oldest of these.
  • Click a date to open its entry, or a column header to sort. The report covers the whole ledger, whatever Fava's filters.
