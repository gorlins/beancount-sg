How to set up (for you, not the reader)
  • Give each account its institution, its owner (or owners, if held jointly) and its number on its open directive. An institution or owner on a parent account applies to its subaccounts. Only the number's last 4 digits are shown: 
    2020-01-01 open Assets:Chase
  institution: "Chase"
  owner: "Joint (Scott and Jane)"

2020-01-01 open Assets:Chase:Checking USD
  account-number: "123456789"
  • Each account is on a line of the statement, inferred from its cash-flow role, its name, and loans opened under the same path (e.g. Liabilities:Home:Mortgage under Assets:Home makes the home real estate). Declare asset-type on an asset account, a parent, or the commodity it holds, or liability-type on a liability, to place one: 
    2020-01-01 open Assets:Jewelry USD
  asset-type: "personal-property"

2020-01-01 commodity HOUSE-MAIN-ST
  asset-type: "real-estate"
    The asset types are {% for line in extension.lines if not line.liability %}{{ line.key }}{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}; the liability types {% for line in extension.lines if line.liability %}{{ line.key }}{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}.
  • Mark each bill: an expense account, or a loan or card the bill pays, with bill: TRUE to use its name, or a name for the reader. Its payments are the transactions adding to the account: 
    2020-01-01 open Expenses:Home:Electric USD
  bill: "Electric (City Power)"
  • The summary covers the whole ledger as of today, whatever Fava's filters. Download the PDF and keep it somewhere your family can find it, and update it now and then.
